# Task or activity, and the day lines in the day menu

*2026-10-27*

A block on the planner can change its mind. And the wake-up and sleep lines moved to the day menu.

## What you can do
- **Turn a task into an activity.** Right-click a task block and pick "Turn into Activity". The time stays on the planner as an activity. The notes of the task go with it. If the task had no other block, the task goes away. If it had subtasks or points, Grove asks first.
- **Turn an activity into a task.** Right-click an activity and pick "Turn into Task", or open the activity and press "Task" under the title. Grove makes a task for that day, as long as the block, with the notes of the activity. The task panel opens on it.
- **From the task panel.** A task with one block shows "Task · Activity" in its Plan section. Press "Activity".
- **One step back.** ⌘Z undoes the switch. The block keeps its linked note.
- **Wake-up and sleep lines.** Right-click a day name in the Planner, or a day in the Calendar (on a phone, press and hold), and pick "Add wake-up line" or "Add sleep line". On Today, use the date at the top. A right-click on empty time still adds a line at that time.

## Also new
The "+" button above the schedule is gone. The header has more room for the dates.

## Not done yet

- A task with more than one block switches one block at a time from the block menu. The task panel shows the switch only for a task with one block.
