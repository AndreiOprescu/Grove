# A menu for each day

*2026-10-20*

Sometimes you want to do something with a whole day, not with one task. "Add a deadline on Friday." "Move everything from today to tomorrow."
Now every day has its own menu.

## What you can do
Right-click a day. On a phone, press and hold it. You can do this in two places:

- the day names at the top of the **Planner** week;
- a day of the **Calendar** month.

The menu shows the date, and then:

- **New event**: an all-day event on that day.
- **New deadline**: the same card, with Deadline already on.
- **New task…**: type one line, like "Call mum 6pm for 20m #home". The task is planned for that day.
- **Open daily note** and **Open weekly note**.
- **Show in Planner** (Calendar only): opens the Planner on that week.
- **Plan my day** (Planner only, today and later days): fills the day's free time with its unplanned tasks, as the button does.
- **Move open tasks to…** (only when the day has open tasks): pick a day. The day's unfinished tasks move there. A time block keeps its time on the new day. Undo puts them all back in one step.

A click and a double-click still do what they did before.
