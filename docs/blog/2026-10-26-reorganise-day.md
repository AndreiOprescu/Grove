# Reorganise the rest of your day

*2026-10-26*

A day rarely goes to plan. Now one button plans what is left of it again.

## What you can do
- **One button.** On today's planner, press the round-arrows button next to "Timeline".
- **Say how much is done.** A window lists today's open tasks, today's tasks with no time yet and today's goal blocks. Drag the bar or type a number from 0 to 100 for each one. As you go, each row shows where it will go: "→ 14:30", "Done" or "Will not fit".
- **What happens.** 100 % ticks the task. A part done keeps the time you worked (the share you set, before now), and the rest becomes a new block later today. The rest is rounded up to 15 minutes: a 60-minute task at 80 % gets a 15-minute block. 0 % moves the whole block.
- **The order.** Tasks with a deadline come first, then goals, then other tasks. In each group, the one with the least time left goes first. Blocks go in free time from now until your sleep line.
- **What does not fit** goes back to today's list. A goal block that does not fit is removed; when part of it is done, the worked part stays as a done block and only the rest is removed.
- **Undo.** Press Apply, and ⌘Z undoes it all in one step.

## Also new
The dock at the top left now has Tasks, Goals and Deadlines. The Notes panel is gone; your notes are on the Notes screen.

## Not done yet

- The rest of a task is one block. It is never split across two gaps.
- A note can no longer be dragged from a panel onto the planner.
