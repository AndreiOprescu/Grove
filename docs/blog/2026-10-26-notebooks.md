# Notebooks for your notes

*2026-10-26*

Notes used to be one long list. Now the Notes screen has a sidebar, so each note has a home.

## What you can do
- **Sections.** The sidebar shows All notes, Inbox (notes in no notebook), Daily, Weekly, Pinned, Meetings (notes linked to an event), With tasks (notes with an open task) and Archive. Each one shows how many notes it has.
- **Notebooks.** Press **+** next to NOTEBOOKS and type a name. Right-click a notebook (or press and hold it on a phone) to rename it, give it a colour, add a section or delete it. Deleting a notebook keeps its notes; ⌘Z brings it back.
- **Sections in a notebook.** Each section has an "Add note to …" button. "New section" at the end makes one more. On a computer, drag a note to sort it or to put it in another section.
- **Move and archive.** Right-click a note and pick "Move to notebook…" or "Archive". The open note has the same two buttons next to the pin. Above its title, the note shows where it lives.
- **Tags.** Your tags are listed under the notebooks.
- **Smaller windows.** Under about 1000 points wide, the sidebar becomes a section button above the list. On a phone, the list has a back button to the sections.

## Before you try it
The owner runs `supabase/migrations/20261026000000_grove_sync_notebooks.sql` once in Supabase, so notebooks sync.
