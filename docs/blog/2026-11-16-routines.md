# Routines: tasks that come back

*2026-11-16*

Some jobs are never finished: water the plants, clean the filter, call your gran. A routine is a task that comes back a set time after you last did it.

## What you can do
- **Turn it on.** Open a task's panel and turn on **Routine**. Pick "every 1 week", "every 3 days" or "every 2 months". The clock starts today.
- **See what is due.** A **Routines** list sits at the top of the Tasks panel. Late routines come first, then the one with the least time left. A bar fills as time runs out: "3 days left", "Due today", and after that it stays full in red with "Late 2 days".
- **Tick it.** A tick means done today. The routine stays in the list and its clock starts again from today. Untick it, or press ⌘Z, and the day before comes back.
- **Plan it.** Drag a routine onto a day in the planner. Ticking the block is the same as ticking the routine.
- **History and rewards.** The panel says "Done 6 times, last on 12 Nov". Each tick pays the task's reward to your skills; an untick takes it back.
- **One reminder.** You get one notification at 09:00 on the due day, and no more after that.

## Good to know
- A routine has no due date and no Repeat. Repeat makes a new copy on fixed dates; a routine waits for you.
- Routines never show up in Overdue, Unscheduled or Done today.
- Turning the switch off keeps the days you did it.
