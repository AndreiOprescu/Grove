## What the planner does

- Hours 00:00 to 24:00. One hour is 64 points. Zoom in and out with the two buttons. The grid opens one hour before "now". A line shows "now" on today.
- Working hours have a tint. Past time on today is faded.
- A drag on empty time makes a new block. It snaps to 15 minutes. A short drag makes 30 minutes. Then you type the title. Enter saves. Escape drops it.
- A drag on a block moves it. A drag to another day column moves it to that day. A label shows the new time during the drag. Nothing is saved before you let go.
- The top and bottom edges change the start and the end. 15 minutes is the least.
- Blocks at the same time sit side by side with an inset. Both titles stay in view.
- Keys: arrows move the block (15 minutes, or one day). Shift + arrow changes the length. Delete removes. Escape clears. N starts a new block. Enter renames a task block. Cmd/Ctrl+Z is Undo. Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z is Redo. Ctrl+Y is Redo on Windows and Android.
- A task block has a check box. A tall task block shows its subtasks, then "+N more". A short one shows a count ("0/2").
- Tasks of the day with no time are sticky notes above the grid. Drag a note onto the grid to give it a time. Drag a task block up to the strip to take its time away. The small button on a note puts it in the next free time.
- "Plan my day" shows a list first. "Apply" makes the blocks. One Undo takes them all away.
- A repeating event asks "This event only" or "All events" before a change.
- The bar in the header shows the planned time against the daily limit. It is red over the limit.

## Mouse and touch

| | Mouse | Finger |
|---|---|---|
| New block | drag on empty time | press, hold, then drag |
| Move a block | drag | press, hold, then drag |
| Change the length | drag an edge | drag an edge |
| Scroll | wheel | swipe |
| Menu of a block | right click | press and hold, no move |
| Rename | double click | double tap |

A swipe with a finger scrolls the planner. That is why a finger must hold first.

## Phone vs desktop

- Narrower than 700 pt: the Planner shows one day column (the plan says so). Previous and Next move one day. The header has two rows.
- 700 pt and wider: the Planner shows the week, 7 columns. Previous and Next move one week.
- The "Today" screen shows the one-day timeline at all widths (at most 720 pt wide).
